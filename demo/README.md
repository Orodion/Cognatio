# Example family tree "Familie Kraft (Demo)"

`Cognatio-Demo-Familie-Kraft.zip` is an export file of Cognatio (4 MB) with a complete example: ten fictional persons in four
generations of a family from Munich, built around the life of **Thomas Kraft** (1951 – 2025). Persons, texts and dates are
invented, the photos are AI generated; any resemblance to real persons is accidental. The content is in German.

## What it shows

| Feature           | In the example                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Persons           | all fields: title, nickname, middle name, birth name, address, phone, e-mail, occupation, hobbies, marital status, nationality, languages                                                                     |
| Dates             | exact (`14.09.1951`), month (`05.1953`), year (`1921`), approximate (`um 1925`) and a range (`1970-1974`)                                                                                                     |
| Relations         | wife/husband with a wedding date and a note, life partner, father/mother, brother/sister, and a free relation with a label ("Taufpate"); the second parent and the siblings of a child are derived by the app |
| Life events       | all categories (birth, baptism, school, wedding, occupation, move, death, other), with place and description                                                                                                  |
| Photos            | 18 photos of Thomas linked to the events of his life, some of them also to events of other persons (wedding, births, family meeting); a portrait as the profile picture                                       |
| Notes             | stories about Thomas, his parents, his wife and the grandson                                                                                                                                                  |
| Mourning ribbon   | three deceased persons (date or place of death)                                                                                                                                                               |
| Family tree views | tree, ancestors, descendants and relationship network with Thomas as the focus person; statistics over four generations                                                                                       |

## Using it

- **Desktop app:** the app creates the family tree itself on the first start, so it does not open without one. It is
  created only once per installation; delete it under _Family trees_ when you want to start with your own data.
- **Any time:** under _Import/Export_ choose the file and import it **as a new family tree**.
